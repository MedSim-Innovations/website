# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of this repository, are committed to fostering a welcoming, safe, and collaborative environment. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age  
- Body size  
- Disability  
- Ethnicity  
- Gender identity and expression  
- Level of experience  
- Nationality  
- Personal appearance  
- Race  
- Religion (or lack thereof)  
- Sexual identity and orientation  
- Technical background  
- Educational background  

We value respectful behavior, inclusive language, and empathy toward all collaborators.


## Our Standards

### Examples of positive behavior include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others  
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences  
- Providing constructive feedback  
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them  
- Prioritizing what is best for the project and community  
- Helping create a collaborative and supportive environment  

### Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments  
- Attempted intimidation or aggressive communication  
- Publishing private information (doxing) without consent  
- Trolling, disruptive behavior, or intentional provocation  
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances  
- Any behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting  


## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Upholding this Code of Conduct  
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior  
- Taking fair and appropriate corrective action when violations occur  
- Removing contributions or comments that violate guidelines  
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors for severe or repeated offenses  


## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (repository discussions, issues, PRs, documentation, etc.)  
- Public interactions related to the project (social media, forums, external communications)  
- Any situation where a contributor represents the project or its community  

Examples of representing the project include using an official email address, posting from an affiliated account, or acting as a delegate during online or offline events.

## Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction
**Impact:** Minor issues or unintended behavior  
**Consequence:** A private reminder about expected standards  

### 2. Warning
**Impact:** A first or repeated violation after a warning  
**Consequence:** A clear, written warning with expectations for improvement  

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Impact:** Serious or repeated misconduct  
**Consequence:** Contributor is temporarily removed from project interactions  

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Impact:** Severe violations (e.g., harassment, doxing, violent threats)  
**Consequence:** Permanent removal from all project spaces
