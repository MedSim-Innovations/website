# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the healthcare context of the company.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [sales@medsiminnovations.com](mailto:sales@medsiminnovations.com). Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
